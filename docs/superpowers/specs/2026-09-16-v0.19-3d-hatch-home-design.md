# Bigimong v0.19 3D Hatch and Focus Home Design

Status: approved section by section in chat on 2026-09-16. This written specification awaits final user review before implementation planning.

## Goal

Replace the current two-dimensional egg/home presentation with a mobile-first Unity experience in which the Bigimong egg, hatched dinosaur, and player avatar are real-time 3D models on one consistent focus stage. Egg care and verified walking both advance a 30,000-point hatch target. Reaching the target enables an explicit hatch button, plays a recoverable shell-breaking sequence, and reveals one persisted random baby dinosaur from the 30-species catalog with its name.

This is the first independently shippable subproject of the wider Bigimong product. It establishes the home-stage, hatch, save, and model-presentation boundaries that the later shop/equipment and AR battle subprojects will reuse.

## Scope

### Included in v0.19

- One Unity focus stage for the egg, baby dinosaur, and avatar.
- Short-tap egg care, a two-hour care cooldown, and future-ready verified-step ingestion.
- A 30,000-point target and a separate `부화하기` button.
- A recoverable first-hatch animation and one uniformly random art ID from 1 through 30.
- The revealed species name and baby growth stage.
- Dinosaur/avatar switching, full horizontal 360-degree touch rotation, and a front-view reset.
- Versioned local persistence, v1 migration, last-good backup recovery, and duplicate-event protection.
- Accepted imported 3D assets first, with actual 3D procedural fallbacks for missing catalog entries.
- Responsive portrait UI, reference-matched typography treatment, device safe areas, and mobile performance gates.

### Deferred subprojects

- New Health Connect permission UX and server-authoritative reconciliation beyond the existing Android integration.
- Shop purchasing, inventory, equipment fitting, and cosmetic previews.
- Detailed AR placement, medallion throwing, auto-walk facing, shared anchoring, and online 1:1 battle synchronization.
- Final visual acceptance of all 90 dinosaur growth-stage models.

The deferred work must consume the interfaces defined here rather than rewrite hatch ownership or home-stage identity.

## Product Decisions

- The selected layout is **A: Focus Stage**. Approximately 70% of the portrait screen is reserved for one large central 3D subject.
- Before hatching, the stage switch is `비기알 | 아바타`. After hatching, it becomes `공룡 | 아바타`.
- The target is exactly 30,000. Progress is clamped to `0...30000`.
- A valid egg-care tap adds 500 progress and starts a two-hour cooldown.
- Verified walking contributes one progress point per accepted step.
- Gifts, play, quests, purchases, feeding, and screen navigation never add hatch progress.
- Reaching 30,000 changes the state to hatch-ready but never hatches automatically. The player must press `부화하기`.
- The random dinosaur is selected once and committed before the first visual hatch frame. Reopening the app can never reroll it.
- The first hatch sequence cannot be skipped.
- The 30 catalog entries have equal selection weight in v0.19. Rarity is not used to alter hatch odds in this subproject.
- The reveal card shows `등급: 기본` for every v0.19 hatch. Varied rarity and weighted odds are deferred to the collection/economy design and may not retroactively reroll an owned art ID.
- Dinosaurs appear in their pure base form with purchasable hats, clothing, glasses, sunglasses, backpacks, and other cosmetics unequipped.
- The home avatar uses only a child-safe neutral basic underlayer. It is part of the base body presentation, is not a purchasable costume, and cannot be unequipped.
- The male avatar already present in the user's VARCO generation list must be imported through its stable asset key; it must not be generated again.
- A summoning medallion is not worn in the home stage. A later AR sequence may create or attach it only for the summon action.
- Character silhouette, proportions, palette, rig, and animation identity remain stable across home and later battle views. Cosmetic systems may attach to defined sockets but may not replace the underlying body.

## Existing-System Changes

The current Unity beta has useful persistence and catalog foundations, but several behaviors are intentionally superseded:

- `OfflineReferenceProgress` currently allows play and gifts to add egg experience. v0.19 removes those hatch-progress side effects.
- `PolishEgg()` currently has no two-hour cooldown. v0.19 moves care rules behind a clock-aware progress service.
- The current reference UI can hatch by pressing the egg again and defaults to art ID 1. v0.19 requires a separate button and a persisted random ID.
- `BigimongReferenceUi` currently treats `egg.jpg` and `home.jpg` as the playable scene. In v0.19 they remain visual references; the subject area becomes a real Unity 3D stage with native responsive controls.
- `CharacterPrefabCatalog`, the VARCO import catalog, and procedural factories remain the model-resolution boundary.
- Existing AR battle, Android host, wallet, and server journey contracts are not rewritten by this subproject.

Where this document conflicts with earlier pilot requirements that showed a dinosaur hat, expedition clothing, backpack, or a permanently worn avatar medallion, this document controls the v0.19 home-stage base appearance.

## Runtime Architecture

### `HatchHomeCoordinator`

Owns the user-visible state machine and is the only component allowed to advance hatch phases:

```text
EGG_ACTIVE -> HATCH_READY -> HATCHING -> REVEAL -> HOME
```

- `EGG_ACTIVE`: progress is below 30,000; the care interaction may be available or cooling down.
- `HATCH_READY`: progress is exactly 30,000; the egg still displays and the explicit hatch button is enabled.
- `HATCHING`: the selected art ID has already been saved; stage input and navigation are locked.
- `REVEAL`: the baby model and name appear after the full-screen transition.
- `HOME`: the normal dinosaur/avatar focus stage is active.

The coordinator consumes narrow interfaces for progress, persistence, model resolution, animation, and presentation. It does not read files, query a step provider, generate random numbers, or instantiate catalog models directly.

### `HatchProgressService`

Applies only two progress-event types:

1. `CareTap`: exactly 500 points when cooldown permits.
2. `VerifiedStepDelta`: a non-negative step delta carrying provider ID, day key, cumulative cursor, and event ID.

Every accepted event is clamped at 30,000, saved, and recorded for idempotence. Duplicate event IDs and provider totals at or below the saved day cursor have no effect. Progress events received after `HATCH_READY` have no effect on the egg.

The service depends on:

- `IWalkingProgressProvider`, which supplies verified deltas and stable event IDs;
- `ICareClock`, which supplies UTC time for cooldown decisions;
- `IHatchHomeStore`, which commits state and the bounded event ledger.

The v0.19 standalone provider accepts only deltas delivered through the existing host/test adapter. The current Android Health Connect path may feed that bridge without changing its permission or server contracts. When no trusted provider is present, the provider returns zero and the UI reports `걸음 연동 준비 중`; it must not fabricate steps from device motion. A later reconciliation adapter can replace the local authority without changing the state machine.

For the offline cooldown, effective time is `max(deviceUtcNow, lastObservedUtc)`. A successful care event sets `nextCareAtUtc` to effective time plus two hours. Rolling the device clock backward cannot reset or shorten the saved cooldown. The local beta is not presented as server-grade anti-cheat; future online play uses server time.

### `HatchHomeStore`

Stores a versioned JSON snapshot under `Application.persistentDataPath` with a primary and redundant current backup. A write is prepared to a temporary file, validated, then atomically promoted to backup and then primary. A nonnegative monotonic `revision` distinguishes a newly committed backup from an older primary after interruption; older v2 saves without this field have revision zero. Before the next write, any newest surviving snapshot is replicated to the other path so it cannot be overwritten as the only current copy. `TrySave(false)` means the candidate was not committed; failures after the candidate backup promotion return true because the candidate is durable even if primary catch-up failed. An adapter exception after atomic promotion is reconciled against the exact validated candidate and revision. If a second I/O failure prevents determining the outcome, a typed exception fences new writes until strict `Load()` succeeds; the coordinator reloads and publishes recovered state before allowing a retry, retaining the lock/retry notice while recovery is blocked. This strict-read rule applies to every store instance, including the first `Load()` after a process restart: an exists/read I/O exception from any present v2 replica or the v1 source fails closed instead of treating that source as absent. Malformed JSON, invalid schema, or invalid identity remains data corruption and may fall back to another valid replica, valid v1 migration, or fresh state.

The v2 snapshot contains at least:

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `schemaVersion` | Exactly `2` after migration. |
| `revision` | Monotonic store-assigned commit revision; absent in older v2 JSON means zero. |
| `phase` | One of the five hatch/home phases. |
| `eggProgress` | Integer 0–29,999 in `EGG_ACTIVE`; exactly 30,000 in every other phase. Raw contradictory v2 snapshots are rejected before normalization. |
| `nextCareAtUtcTicks` | Zero before first care or the next allowed UTC instant. |
| `lastObservedUtcTicks` | Monotonic local cooldown floor. |
| `selectedArtId` | `0` before hatch selection; 1 through 30 after selection. |
| `hatchCheckpoint` | `NONE`, `STARTED`, `SHELL_BURST`, or `REVEALED`. |
| `hatchedAtUtcTicks` | Zero until reveal, then immutable. |
| `activeHomeView` | `EGG`, `DINOSAUR`, or `AVATAR`, normalized against phase. |
| `stepProviderCursors` | Highest accepted cumulative total per provider/day so old totals cannot be replayed. |
| `processedStepEvents` | The 256 most recent event IDs for duplicate protection. |

Existing coins, snacks, dragon experience, daily counters, sound preference, and avatar profile remain preserved even though they do not affect hatch progress in this subproject.

### Migration from the current v1 save

- Preserve unhatched `eggExperience` as `eggProgress`, clamped at 30,000.
- Preserve coins, snacks, dragon experience, daily counters, sound preference, and existing avatar data.
- If `hatched == true`, preserve the clamped old `selectedArtId`, use `HOME`, mark the reveal complete, and set completed progress to 30,000. Only this explicit legacy ownership migration may lift progress; contradictory v2 ownership is never inferred by normalization.
- If not hatched and progress is 30,000, use `HATCH_READY` and keep `selectedArtId = 0` so the first explicit hatch remains random.
- Otherwise use `EGG_ACTIVE` and `selectedArtId = 0`.
- Migration is written only after the v1 source has been read successfully and the v2 snapshot validates. The v1 file remains available as the first recovery source.

Load chooses the newest valid v2 revision across primary and backup (primary wins only on equal revision), then a valid v1 migration, then a fresh state. The backup already contains the new selection when it is accepted, before any later hatch checkpoint. Recovery must never silently turn an already persisted hatch selection into a new random draw. If the coordinator's initial load fails on I/O, it completes initialization and subscriptions with a render-only safe placeholder, exposes only input lock plus retry, and does not publish that placeholder as durable state. `RetryHatch()` retries the strict load; once storage recovers, the coordinator publishes the newest durable snapshot and resumes its normal presentation.

### `HatchSelectionService`

Uses an injectable random source to choose an integer in the inclusive range 1 through 30 with equal probability. Pressing `부화하기` performs one logical transaction:

1. Confirm state is `HATCH_READY` and progress is 30,000.
2. Select the art ID.
3. Resolve the species name from the single Unity species catalog.
4. Save the art ID, `HATCHING`, and checkpoint `STARTED`.
5. Begin visual playback only after save success.

If the commit fails, the screen remains `HATCH_READY`, the button unlocks, and a retry message appears. A random result is never shown before it is durable.

### `HomeFocusStage`

Maintains one camera, lighting rig, ground shadow, stage root, and active subject slot. It instantiates only one of the egg, dinosaur, or avatar at a time. Switching subjects fades the outgoing model, releases it, loads the next subject, and fades it in without recreating the whole screen.

Model resolution order is:

1. accepted imported prefab for the stable key and growth stage;
2. registered generated prefab for the same art ID;
3. actual 3D procedural fallback matching that art ID's body plan and palette.

The egg resolves through stable key `Bigimong_Egg`. The revealed dinosaur always requests the `BABY`/`아동기` stage. An absent model produces a development warning and a 3D fallback, never a blank stage or a two-dimensional character card. A fallback is functional beta coverage and does not count as final visual acceptance.

All optional cosmetic renderers and socket children start disabled. The avatar's neutral underlayer remains enabled. The home stage never permanently mutates the source prefab, so later equipment and AR presentation can use the same identity safely.

## Focus-Stage Presentation

### Layout

- The 3D subject occupies the central visual area and remains clear of top currency/status controls and the bottom action/navigation region.
- Before hatch, the segmented switch reads `비기알 | 아바타`; after hatch it reads `공룡 | 아바타`.
- Egg progress is always shown numerically as `current / 30,000` and with a bar.
- Below 30,000, the primary button is visibly disabled and shows the remaining requirement.
- At 30,000, the primary button becomes gold, gently pulses, and reads `부화하기`.
- During hatching, stage switching, navigation, care, and hatch controls are locked.
- On reveal, the species plate shows two-digit art ID, Korean species name, `아동기`, and `등급: 기본`. It remains visible in normal dinosaur home view.
- Safe-area padding applies to camera cutouts, rounded corners, and gesture/navigation insets.

### Visual language and typography

The supplied `egg.jpg` and `home.jpg` remain the visual authority for color, rounded shapes, orange-gold buttons, cream highlights, dark cocoa outlines, and high-contrast Korean lettering. New text uses bundled Noto Sans KR Black/Bold TextMeshPro assets and one `BigimongTypeStyle` definition rather than per-screen fonts. The logo remains approved artwork; interactive Korean text uses the Black weight for large names/actions and Bold for counters/body text, with the reference's cream or white face, dark cocoa outline, and soft drop shadow. The font atlas includes the shipped Korean strings, numerals, and required Latin labels rather than an unrestricted dynamic CJK atlas. Weight, outline, shadow, spacing, and number formatting are validated against reference screenshots; no unrelated font is introduced on a single screen.

The central 3D subject uses soft toy-like materials, warm key light, cool fill, a subtle rim, and a stable ground shadow. Camera distance and model framing are stored per asset without changing its authored body proportions. Low-quality mode may reduce shadow, particles, antialiasing, texture mip, or render scale, but must never deform the character or substitute a different silhouette.

## Touch Interaction

Input arbitration happens before gameplay actions:

- A press that begins over a UI control belongs to the UI and never rotates or cares for the egg.
- A press on the 3D subject lasting no more than 0.22 seconds with total motion below 18 density-independent pixels is a tap.
- Motion reaching 18 dp becomes a drag and permanently cancels the tap for that pointer sequence.
- Horizontal drag rotates the subject freely through 360 degrees around its stage-local vertical axis.
- Small vertical motion is ignored for camera orbit; the beta does not tilt beneath the floor or expose unbuilt model interiors.
- Release may apply at most 0.35 seconds of damped rotational inertia.
- A small `정면 보기` control restores the asset's authored front heading.
- Pinch zoom is excluded from v0.19 to prevent clipping, inconsistent framing, and accidental activation.

A valid egg tap requests care and plays the care reaction only after the progress service accepts it. A cooldown tap gives a short harmless wobble and displays the remaining time without awarding progress. Dinosaur and avatar taps play short idle reactions but never add hatch progress.

## Hatch Sequence

The first hatch lasts approximately five seconds and uses persisted checkpoints rather than frame-by-frame save writes.

| Time | Checkpoint and presentation |
| ---: | --- |
| 0.0–0.7 s | `STARTED`: the egg rocks with rising sound and optional light haptics. |
| 0.7–1.8 s | Three visible crack stages appear with synchronized sound and restrained camera impulse. |
| 1.8–2.4 s | Persist `SHELL_BURST`; shell pieces burst outward with child-friendly particles. |
| 2.4–3.2 s | Warm light and the selected baby's silhouette replace the egg. |
| 3.2–4.2 s | Transition from the hatch presentation into the same focus-stage camera. |
| 4.2–5.0 s | Persist `REVEALED`; baby idle begins and the art ID/name/`아동기` plate appears. |

After the reveal presentation finishes, the coordinator automatically enters `HOME`; the species plate remains. There is no first-hatch skip control.

If the app is interrupted:

- `STARTED` replays from the beginning with the already saved art ID.
- `SHELL_BURST` resumes at the warm-light/reveal portion without reconstructing an intact selectable egg.
- `REVEALED` opens the dinosaur home stage directly.

Audio, vibration, particles, and camera impulse respect platform settings. Disabling sound must not alter animation timing or state commits.

The saved scene owns one non-autoplay 2D AudioSource and a runtime binding that generates the bounded deterministic rock/crack/burst PCM cues on load. Cues are reused across retries and destroyed with their owner; no transient editor-created AudioClip reference is required to survive scene serialization. Saved sound preference and explicit presentation mute gate playback without overriding AudioSource mute/volume or AudioListener settings.

## Error Handling

- **Save failure before hatch:** remain hatch-ready, show a retry message, and do not start animation.
- **Save failure at a later checkpoint:** keep the previous durable checkpoint; on restart replay from that point with the same art ID.
- **Missing imported model:** show the registered 3D procedural fallback with the same art ID, body plan, palette, stage, and Korean name.
- **Step provider unavailable:** retain saved progress, show `걸음 연동 준비 중`, and keep care available according to its cooldown.
- **Duplicate or negative step event:** ignore it without changing progress.
- **Corrupt primary save:** load the last-good snapshot or valid migration source and show a non-blocking recovery notice.
- **Unreadable save or migration source:** fail closed even on a new process/store instance; show only a locked retry state until strict reload succeeds.
- **Unknown phase or out-of-range value:** normalize only when identity is unambiguous; otherwise use the last-good snapshot rather than inventing ownership.
- **Model switch failure:** keep or restore the previous active subject and re-enable navigation.
- **Low memory:** release the outgoing model and transient hatch effects before retrying the requested subject at reduced visual quality.

The core egg, hatch, reveal, 360-degree review, and avatar switch remain usable without a network connection.

## Mobile Performance Budget

- Target 60 frames per second on a representative mid-range Android device; never remain below 30 fps during normal home interaction.
- Input-to-visible-response target is under 100 ms.
- Warm subject switching target is under two seconds.
- Resident memory target is at most 350 MB in steady home and 450 MB at hatch peak.
- Only one home subject renderer and Animator are active at once.
- Production character budgets continue to follow the existing pilot ceiling: at most 30,000 rendered triangles, a 1024×1024 primary atlas, four material slots, and 64 deform bones per character.
- Generate/use LOD1 near 55% of accepted triangles. The stage chooses LOD by projected size and device quality without changing identity.
- Pool short-lived hatch particles and shell pieces; do not leave hidden particle systems, cameras, lights, or Animators active after reveal.
- Prefer baked/reflection-probe environment lighting and one restrained real-time key light. Low quality disables expensive shadow/particle features before lowering model integrity.

Performance claims require an Android player profile or device capture. Editor frame rate alone is not acceptance evidence.

## Verification

### Deterministic data and migration tests

- 29,999 is not hatch-ready; exactly 30,000 is hatch-ready.
- Accepted care adds exactly 500, clamps at 30,000, and rejects attempts during the two-hour cooldown.
- Play, gifts, quests, purchases, feeding, and navigation leave egg progress unchanged.
- Verified step deltas apply once; duplicate IDs, stale cumulative cursors, negative deltas, and post-ready deltas do not change progress.
- All 30 art IDs are reachable through an injected deterministic random source and each selected ID is saved before presentation.
- Restart at every hatch checkpoint preserves the same art ID and follows the defined resume path.
- Valid v1 saves migrate without resetting hatch progress, currency, inventory counters, dragon experience, sound preference, avatar data, or an already hatched art ID.
- Corrupt-primary recovery uses the last-good snapshot and never rerolls a durable selection.
- A restarted store cannot return stale primary or fresh state while a present newer v2 replica or v1 source is unreadable; retry publishes the newest literal payload without rerolling or losing care/step state.

### Unity behavior tests

- Tap/drag thresholds distinguish care from rotation at the 0.22-second and 18-dp boundaries.
- A drag can expose front, both sides, and rear through full horizontal rotation.
- `정면 보기` returns to the authored heading.
- UI touches do not reach stage input.
- Pre-hatch and post-hatch segmented controls expose only valid subjects.
- Only one subject renderer/Animator remains active after repeated switching.
- Imported prefab resolution takes precedence and every missing entry resolves to a true 3D fallback.
- All optional cosmetic sockets/renderers start unequipped; the avatar neutral underlayer remains.
- Hatch controls remain locked during animation and unlock only in the correct final state.

### Visual and device acceptance

- Capture egg, hatch-ready, crack, burst, reveal, dinosaur-front/side/rear, and avatar-front/side/rear screens.
- Review the captures at 16:9, 19.5:9, and 20:9 portrait ratios with safe-area overlays.
- Compare typography, outline, shadow, button color, spacing, and subject scale to the supplied reference images.
- Profile representative low-, mid-, and high-tier Android devices, including at least one 4 GB device compatible with minSdk 28.
- Confirm 60 fps target/30 fps floor, memory ceilings, subject-switch time, and touch response.
- Force missing model, offline, save corruption, app termination at each checkpoint, and low-memory recovery cases.

Passing code tests or loading a procedural fallback does not mean a character's final art is approved. Each accepted production model still requires multi-angle and in-game visual review against its stable art ID reference.

## Delivery Boundary

The v0.19 implementation is complete when:

1. The new state/store/progress boundaries and v1 migration pass automated checks.
2. The focus stage presents a real 3D egg, baby dinosaur, and avatar with approved touch behavior.
3. A 30,000-point state enables a durable random hatch, recoverable animation, correct name, and baby home state.
4. All 30 art IDs resolve to either an accepted model or a visibly distinct 3D fallback.
5. The reference-matched responsive UI and Android performance/error gates pass.
6. Existing AR battle and online contracts remain regression-clean.
7. A Unity Android ARM64 build succeeds and launches on a test phone.

The subsequent shop/equipment spec will attach cosmetics to the stable bodies and default-empty socket state defined here. The subsequent AR battle spec will reuse the same resolved character identity while adding transient summoning equipment, anchored placement, auto-facing, and opponent synchronization.
